Sonara — Space theme: image credits and licences
================================================

The Space theme's skies are built from real observatory imagery, regraded to one look (neutral
blacks, natural star colour, restrained saturation), cut and composited for Sonara by
scripts/space/bake_space_assets.py. Each source is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the
changes made are: cropping, scaling, colour regrading, removing point stars from the panorama,
vignetting and compositing over procedural stars.

Milky Way (the sky the black hole lenses, on Home and the Player)
  "The Milky Way panorama" (eso0932a) — ESO/S. Brunier — CC BY 4.0
  https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/

Library: the Whirlpool Galaxy (M51)
  heic0506a — NASA, ESA, S. Beckwith (STScI) and The Hubble Heritage Team (STScI/AURA) — CC BY 4.0
  https://esahubble.org/images/heic0506a/

Search: the Hubble Ultra Deep Field
  heic0406a — NASA, ESA, S. Beckwith (STScI) and the HUDF Team — CC BY 4.0
  https://esahubble.org/images/heic0406a/

Explore: "Cosmic Cliffs" in the Carina Nebula (regraded to teal, violet and rose)
  weic2205a — NASA, ESA, CSA, and STScI — CC BY 4.0
  https://esawebb.org/images/weic2205a/

The black hole, its accretion disk, the photon ring, the stars over the plates, the planet's
limb, the disk texture and the app icon are Sonara's own: rendered by an original geodesic ray
tracer (scripts/space/trace_blackhole.py) and procedural code, and offered under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The same credits are shown in the app: Settings > Appearance > Space effects > Image credits.
